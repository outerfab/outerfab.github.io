Crowd Supply launch support

You have a hardware project. Turning it into a product also means finding customers, running a campaign and getting it delivered. We can help with every phase, so you can keep focusing on the hardware.

Before launch

We work with you to decide what to sell, who it is for and what it will cost to make and deliver.

We take care of the practical setup, budgets and paperwork, and coordinate legal compliance requirements with the relevant specialists.

We help turn your demos, photos and development notes into communication people can follow.

Set up the campaign

We help prepare the project page, product descriptions, comparison table, photos and video outline. We work through the campaign requirements with you and the Crowd Supply team.

We plan the funding goal, product options, delivery schedule and updates around actual costs and production capacity. That includes what happens if demand is higher than expected.

Run the campaign

We can coordinate launch communication, write regular updates, follow up with relevant press and communities, and handle day-to-day backer questions. We bring technical questions to you and turn your answers into useful information for everyone.

As the campaign progresses, we keep the page, backer communication and production plan aligned. If something changes, we help explain what happened and what comes next.

Make it and deliver it

We can coordinate suppliers, assembly, testing, kitting and packing, and bulk shipment to Crowd Supply for backer fulfilment. For silicon projects, we also arrange chip-on-board bonding and other processing.

After the campaign, we can help with production updates, replenishment and the next batch. The plan should cover ongoing sales as well as the first delivery.

Where do we start?

Send us a short description, a link to your project and where you are now. A working demo, existing audience or initial cost estimate helps.

You can bring us in for a specific phase or ask us to coordinate the whole campaign.

Contact us to discuss your project.
