Known-good die sourcing

We look for bare die to match a requested part, function and quantity.

What it is

Known-good die (KGD) are bare die tested at wafer level and sold as working. Memory parts such as EEPROM and SPI NOR are examples of die we have experience in sourcing.

  • We find a source for the part and the quantity you need, and tell you what it costs per die.
  • When sourcing die we obtain the releveant information about the die and can share this with you.
  • We can send you complete wafers or handle the backgrinding, dicing and division for you as required.

What we need from you

ItemWhy
Part number or functionDescribe the part, use and key requirements.
QuantitySources have minimum wafer or lot sizes.
Die formatWhole wafer or waffle pack.
Wire and pad metalRelevant if you also need chip-on-board bonding.
DeadlineKeep in mind that wafer lead times can vary significantly.

Limits

  • We can only source parts a supplier is willing to sell bare. Many are not.
  • Stock and pricing move. A quote is valid for the period it states, not indefinitely.
  • The documentation is whatever the source provides. We do not create wafer maps or lot data ourselves.
  • We do not source restricted parts. See export compliance.

Questions

Can you source a specific part?

If you need to source "off the shelf" bare die for your projects, let us know what you are looking for and we can help source these.

Do the die come tested?

Known-good die come tested at wafer level by the source. We describe in the quote what the source tested and what it did not.

Can you bond them onto my board?

Yes as part or our chip-on-board service
