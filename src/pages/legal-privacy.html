Privacy notice

We use the details you provide to handle quote requests and enquiries. This site sets no cookies and shows no advertising. We do not sell your details.

Who we are

Outerfab is a service brand run by a team in Shenzhen. To ask us about data handled through this site, contact us.

What we collect, and why

WhereWhatWhy
Quote request formEmail, name, selected service, specifications, project notes and request referenceTo answer you
Site traffic, when enabledPage views and referrers through Cloudflare Web AnalyticsTo understand site visits

If you subscribe to Outerfab news and updates, we collect your email address through Formspark to send those updates.

Who handles it

  • Formspark records quote requests and delivers submissions to us by email.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Its Web Analytics beacon is included only when enabled in the site configuration.
  • Google Workspace holds our email and files, including your correspondence with us.

Your details are handled by our team and these providers wherever they operate. For a job, relevant technical and delivery information may be shared with the partners doing the work. Contact details are shared only when needed for the job and with your agreement.

Cookies

None. The site has no cookie banner because it sets no cookies. Fonts are served from our own domain.

How long we keep it

Quote and contact correspondence for as long as the job or conversation needs, and afterwards for as long as our accounts and any warranty need.
