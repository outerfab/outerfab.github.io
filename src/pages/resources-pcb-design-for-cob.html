PCB design for chip-on-board wire bonding

Design a clear route from each die pad to its PCB bond pad. Keep wires short and uncrossed, leave room for the bonding tool and share the complete panel outline for review before ordering boards.

Wire bond design rules

Place PCB bond pads around the die attach pad so each wire has a clear route.

  • No crossing. Wires must not cross each other.
  • Angle. Aim for each wire to leave the die at no more than 45° from straight out. It is a guide, not a hard limit: a steeper wire may be acceptable, and we flag any that concern us in the review. Pads near die corners tend to produce the longest and steepest wires; plan the PCB pad positions with those corners in mind.
  • Maximum length. Under 3 mm. Keep wires as short as practical; even 3 mm adds inductance.
  • Minimum length. 1 mm from the die, or twice the die thickness, whichever is greater.

Pad pitch

PCB bond pad pitch should be over 200 µm so the tool can land without touching the neighbouring pad.

Pad metal and wire

Aluminium wire at 1.0 mil is our standard and suits the aluminium pads on GF180MCU die. Aluminium wire thickness down to 0.6mil diameter are available. Gold wire thermosonic bonding is also possible.

Leaving room for the bonding tool

Your other components are normally assembled before the die goes on, so they are already on the board while the machine works. The bonding tool comes down onto each pad from above and needs space. A component that is too close or too tall can block it.

  • Zone 1, up to 2 mm beyond the pads: no components.
  • Zone 2, 2 to 5 mm: low-profile parts only, about 1 mm tall or less, such as 0402 or 0603 passives.
  • Beyond 5 mm: taller parts such as connectors, pin headers, crystals and electrolytic capacitors.

These clearances are recommended starting points. If your layout cannot meet them, send it to us and we will check it before ordering PCBs.

Pads, finish and mask

ItemRule
Surface finishENIG or ENEPIG surface finish on the PCB. No solderpaste on the bond pads.
Solder maskOpen the mask fully over the bond pads and the die attach pad. No mask or silkscreen on the pads themselves. At least 6 mil (150 µm) clearance around the pads.
Die attach padFlat. Avoid open vias under the die, or have them filled and capped, so the adhesive does not wick away. Make the pad 0.3 mm larger than the die on every side (0.6 mm wider and taller in total).
UndersideComponents on the back are acceptable, including under the bonding area, but a flat, clear underside is preferable. Boards have been bonded with a connector and capacitors beneath the die, on a jig with slots cut to clear them. If you have underside parts, tell us their heights and we make a jig to suit.

Arranging boards into a panel

The bonding partner confirms the panel format, working edges, tooling and fiducial positions for the selected process and jig. Share the full panel outline and board thickness before ordering. Arrange every board in the same orientation; routed slots may help with small boards. Panels should have rails on each side to ensure they are held securely during the bonding process.
